# study planner 

**A little digital café for getting your shit together.**

A study planner I made because I wanted all the annoying little parts of studying in one place instead of having my tasks in one app, notes somewhere else, resources in random tabs, and a timer on my phone.

It's basically a productivity app with a café theme running through it.

## what it has

### 📋 planner

The main part of the app.

* Add study tasks
* Add subjects
* Set estimated time for tasks
* Keep track of what's done and what's left
* See your progress
* Keep everything in one place

### ⏱️ focus timer

A built-in Pomodoro timer for when it's time to actually do the work.

* Focus sessions
* Breaks
* Session tracking
* Sound effects
* Coffee-break rewards

### 📝 notes

A separate place for study notes so they don't get mixed in with everything else.

* Create and organize notes
* Search notes
* Add subjects
* Basic formatting
* Word and character counts

### 🔖 resources

For all the study stuff that would otherwise end up in 37 browser tabs.

* PDFs
* Links
* Videos
* Flashcards
* Other study material
* Subjects and tags
* Search and filtering

### ☕ progress & coffee breaks

The app keeps track of your study activity and gives you coffee breaks as a little reward for getting things done.

Because if I'm making a productivity app, obviously there's going to be coffee.

## why coffee?

I wanted the app to feel a little less like a productivity spreadsheet.

So the café theme runs through the whole thing — the UI, sounds, task language, progress and rewards.

That's basically it.
